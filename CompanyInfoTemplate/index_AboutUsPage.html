About Onstitute

Who are we?

Onstitute is an online education provider company with a special focus on computer- and data-science courses. Our motivation is to spread high quality data-science education to meet the unprecedented demand of data-experts across the globe. We are commited to our students to ace the skill and become project-ready from absolute beginner. We also help instructors to design their courses to save time and improve learning.

Our Mission

Our mission is to teach the students and help the instructors to teach cutting edge data-science courses to make students from beginner to project-ready in significantly less time than they think. Our scientific teaching techniques, industry exposure, course materials full with hands-on on cutting edge hardware infrastructure at the lowest cost guarantee the students mastering the skills that the industry demands in weeks instead of months

Our Vision

Our vision is to make tech-education affordable, accessible, and available at anywhere, anytime to anyone. At Onstitute, we not only build courses but, we build a learning community including individuals, industry and academic organizations, and infrastructures that is aiming to meet the technology-learning needs of students across the globe by empowering innovation in education and research.
